## Typography — Manrope + JetBrains Mono

- **Display and body:** Manrope Variable
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono Variable

Use Manrope with generous line-height and slightly tight tracking for hero copy. Use the mono face for room IDs, live status, and small metadata.

## Gradients

Gradients are restrained and reserved for brand moments:

```css
--gradient-bg: linear-gradient(135deg, #FCF5EC 0%, #F5E9E1 100%);
--gradient-accent: linear-gradient(to right, #B94F32 0%, #C9784F 100%);
```

## Tone and voice

Write like a thoughtful host: warm, direct, and specific. Focus on the shared moment rather than technical complexity.

Avoid hype, urgency, corporate language, and exclamation marks. Prefer “Start a room” over “Unlock a powerful experience.”

## Usage

- Use semantic CSS variables from `index.css`; do not hardcode colors in components.
- Keep surfaces warm and quiet, with Peach Cinema as the single brand accent.
- Use real buttons and links with visible focus states.
- Keep the landing page spacious and editorial; reserve density for the watching room.
